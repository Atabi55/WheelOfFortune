Wheel of Fortune demo project. <br>
Features: <br>
3 reward pools; Bronze, Silver and Gold. <br>
Implemented with ScriptableObjects. <br>
Type and amount of rewards in the pools can be easily modified from the editor. <br>
Milestone display on the right showing next safe and super zones. <br>
Sliding spin counter on the top to keep track of number of spins and show upcoming spin types. <br>
List of pending rewards on the left. Player can choose to claim rewards by exiting. <br>
Rewards on the wheel, wheel and indicator sprites change based on spin type. <br>
