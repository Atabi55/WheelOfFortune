Wheel of Fortune demo project.
Features:
3 reward pools; Bronze, Silver and Gold.
Implemented with ScriptableObjects.
Type and amount of rewards in the pools can be easily modified from the editor.
Milestone display on the right showing next safe and super zones.
Sliding spin counter on the top to keep track of number of spins and show upcoming spin types.
List of pending rewards on the left. Player can choose to claim rewards by exiting.
Rewards on the wheel, wheel and indicator sprites change based on spin type.
